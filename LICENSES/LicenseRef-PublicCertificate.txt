LicenseRef-PublicCertificate

This project redistributes, unmodified, public certificates and timestamp tokens issued by third parties, solely so
that anyone can verify the project's timestamps offline:

- stamps/roots/*.pem: public root certificates of the timestamp authorities (DigiCert Trusted Root G4; USERTrust RSA
  Certification Authority; Free TSA Root CA), as published by their issuers;
- stamps/*/*.tsr: RFC 3161 timestamp responses issued by DigiCert, Inc., Sectigo Limited and Free TSA for this
  project's manifests;
- stamps/*/*.ots: OpenTimestamps proofs returned by the public calendar servers a.pool.opentimestamps.org and
  b.pool.opentimestamps.org for this project's manifests.

These files belong to their issuers. This project grants no licence to them and makes no claim to them; they are
included only as data needed to check the timestamps (for example with `openssl ts -verify` against the pinned
roots, or the project's `make stamp-verify`). Any rights in them are those of their issuers, under the issuers' own
terms.
