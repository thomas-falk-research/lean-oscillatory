## 1. The question
CONTRIBUTING step 5 says: "A BLOCKER or MAJOR means a new statement (back to 2), never an edit." In batch API3 (ten
statements, `OscInt/Statements/Family.lean`, stamp `20261108T002603Z-9d03130c8f3a`) both blind pre-proof referees judged
every statement FAITHFUL and TRUE and raised a MAJOR against the **sanity theorems only**: they restated hypotheses in
their own words, so a plant in a statement body would have left them green (lessons 11, 33). The lead answered with
sanity theorems that take the statement constant as a hypothesis ("conditional sanity theorems") instead of a re-freeze.
The adversarial referee (`review/2026-11-08-API3-adversarial.md`, M3) called this a departure from the written rule.
Ten STATUS rows say "owner decision pending" until you decide.

## 3. The options

### (i) Ratify an amended step 5 (prospective), with a recorded waiver for API3
**Exact text.** Replace the last two sentences of step 5 in `CONTRIBUTING.md` (from "Reports go verbatim" to "never an
edit.") by:

> Reports go verbatim under `review/`. A BLOCKER or MAJOR against what a statement says means a new statement (back to
> 2), never an edit. **Test-layer exception.** A MAJOR whose subject is only the sanity theorems (they restate a
> hypothesis, use degenerate witnesses, or do not separate a feature), and not the meaning or truth of any statement,
> may be answered without a re-freeze when all of the following hold:
> (a) each referee's report gives every statement of the batch a FAITHFUL, FAITHFUL-STRONGER or FAITHFUL-WEAKER verdict
> and raises no BLOCKER or MAJOR against any statement text, and neither referee (asked, if its report is silent)
> objects to the finding being treated as test-layer only;
> (b) every statement constant of the batch has at least one sanity theorem that takes the constant itself as a
> hypothesis and applies it to data separating each feature the finding names (generic data, lessons 17 and 33);
> (c) for every statement, at least one planted copy of its frozen text (a mutation that strengthens a hypothesis or
> weakens the conclusion) is rejected by these theorems and the unplanted copy is accepted, recorded under
> `docs/replay/plants/`;
> (d) the answer file lists, per statement, which kinds of plants the new theorems cannot detect (in particular any
> plant that weakens a hypothesis) and says that these are left to the fidelity reviews;
> (e) the answer, the sanity files and the plant record are committed and covered by a stamp (`make stamp`) **before
> any proof of the batch is drafted**, in the repository or in scratch;
> (f) the post-proof referees are given the finding and the answer, and the adversarial referee states whether (a)–(e)
> held.
> If the lead and a referee disagree on whether a finding concerns only the test layer, or if any of (a)–(e) fails, the
> rule above applies: a new statement. Each use of this exception is recorded in the batch's STATUS rows.

**Waiver for API3 (one sentence in `docs/STATUS.md` and the API3 answers):** "API3 used the test-layer exception
before it existed and met (a), (d) and (f), but (b) and (c) only after the proofs (5 of 10 constants and 2 of 10
plants before them, all after); ratified by the owner on <date> as a one-time waiver."
**Consequences.** The ten rows lose "owner decision pending" and keep a visible waiver note. Future batches get a
cheaper path for a pure test-layer finding, with conditions that a referee can check. Risk: the exception is a new way
around a re-freeze; the guard against misuse is (a) (both referees must agree the statements are faithful), the
default in the last sentence, and (f). Cost now: one docs commit (CONTRIBUTING, STATUS ×10, answers, HANDOFF).

### (ii) Re-freeze the ten texts as API3′ with bundled hypothesis `def`s
**What it means.** A new file `OscInt/Statements/Family2.lean` with the same ten statements, each hypothesis a named
`def` shared with its sanity theorem (lessons 11), new constants (`…Statement₂`), new stamp, new pins, two blind
pre-proof reviews, the existing proofs re-used by short bridges (`theorem x₂ : X₂Statement := x` after unfolding),
post-proof review, verify-all (~94 min). The API3 constants stay and are superseded under `docs/policy.md`.
**Consequences.** The written rule is kept without exception. But "reviewed before any proof" cannot be claimed for
API3′: proofs of the same content exist (a re-freeze of an already proved statement buys rule compliance, not an
order claim). Cost: a full batch cycle (half a session), twenty more constants in `stamps/statements.json`, and two
names for the same theorem for downstream users until the old ones are removed.

## 4. Recommendation
**(i)**, with the waiver worded as above. Reasons: the defect the referees found was in the tests, both referees judged
every statement faithful before and after the proofs, the substitute is strictly stronger than def-bundling for the
plants it covers (it tests the frozen text itself), and (ii) costs half a session without making any claim stronger.
The waiver must not be hidden: (b) and (c) were met only after the proofs, and the STATUS rows should say so.

If you choose (i): apply the text in §3 to `CONTRIBUTING.md` step 5, add the waiver sentence to the ten STATUS rows
in place of "owner decision pending", and to `review/2026-11-08-API3-answers.md` under M3. These are docs-only changes;
`stamps/statements.json` does not change, so the v0.4.0 pins in `docs/release-notes/v0.4.0.md` stay valid.
If you choose (ii): say so, and the next session starts with API3′ before any new batch; v0.4.0 then waits for it.
