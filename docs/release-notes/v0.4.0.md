# OscInt v0.4.0 — release notes (prepared by the lead; the owner publishes)

A minor release (before 1.0.0 the minor version may change statements; this one changes none): 25 new statements, all
proved, in four batches: front-end batch 3 (compact families, the classical fold hypothesis, local smoothness; ten,
API3), batch GD (global decay from a local classification, sharpness, torus unfolding, the 2D lattice-Laplacian

  `LatticeKernel1ExplicitStatement`, T6F `LatticeKernelFactorStatement`, T6E `LatticeLaplacianExplicitStatement`;
- batch VT (`OscInt/Statements/Vector.lean`, stamp `stamps/20261108T235416Z-961cebc296ef`): GD-C-V
  `GlobalDecayCritVecStatement`, GD-V `GlobalDecayVecStatement`, GD-F-V `GlobalFoldVecStatement`, GD-N-V
  `GlobalNondegVecStatement`.

Notes that travel with the release:
- **T6 and T6L are classical.** The 2D lattice kernel factorises into two 1D kernels `2π iⁿ Jₙ(t)` (machine-checked as
  T6F), so T6 is the classical t^{-1/3} Bessel bound squared (best constant: Landau 2000) and T6L the Cauchy–Watson
  asymptotic of `Jₙ(n)`. They are instances of the global theorem, not evidence for non-separable symbols
  (`docs/prior_art.md`).
- **The explicit constants of LE are weak.** They beat the trivial bounds only for t > 518 (1D) and t > 1321 (2D).
- **No non-separable model is covered yet.** The candidate two-band model (QWZ, m = 3) has exact cusp points
  (`docs/design/vector-amplitudes.md` §6).
- **Process.** Batch 3 answered a test-layer MAJOR and batch LE a documentation-only MAJOR of their pre-proof reviews
  without a re-freeze; the owner ratified both as one-time waivers of CONTRIBUTING step 5 on 2026-11-09 (option (iii) of
  `docs/decisions/2026-11-08-step5-memo.md`; steps 2 and 5 amended). Recorded on each STATUS row.
