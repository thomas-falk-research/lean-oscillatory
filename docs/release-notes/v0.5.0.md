# OscInt v0.5.0 — release notes (prepared by the lead; the owner publishes)

A minor release. Before 1.0.0 a minor release may change statements; this one changes none. It adds 11 statements:
- **seven proved and machine-checked as stated,** in three batches:
  - batch A3: the k-th derivative van der Corput bound and the uniform cusp bounds, t^{−3/4};
  - batch LE2: the cube-root constant for the 1D and 2D lattice kernels;
  - batch HC: the d-dimensional hypercubic lattice kernel;
- **four stated, frozen and reviewed but not proved:** batch LS, the free lattice Schrödinger group. Their
  `proved_by` is `null`.

There are 63 statements in all, 59 of them machine-checked as stated. Toolchain `leanprover/lean4:v4.35.0`, Mathlib
`56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679`, both unchanged from v0.4.0.

**Status of these notes: PREPARED.** The pins below were computed on the release branch's tree on 2026-11-13.
`release-candidate` re-checks them on the release commit (`tools/release_preflight.sh`) and refuses to proceed if
they differ.

## Statement changes

tag on 2026-11-13. The `statement_files` list gained four files; its ten existing entries keep their SHA-256 and now name the newest
stamp, as `docs/policy.md` prescribes. Added:

  - S5.1′ `CuspBoundGenStatement` (`OscInt.API.cuspBoundGen`);

  theorem is a minor change). Until then, do not depend on them: `tools/oscint_check.py --statement` on any of them
  fails with "stated but not proved in this OscInt".

Notes that travel with the release:
- **AI-assisted proofs, disclosed.**
  - The hand proofs behind LE2 and A3, and the Lean proofs of the A3 parts and of S1.k, were drafted with an
    external reviewer's OpenAI model.
  - The lead integrated and checked them, and they went through the full trust chain.
  - Tool names and versions, as far as the record supports them, are in `docs/prior_art.md`.
  - The lead, the proof agents (for example those that proved the LE2 parts) and every referee are Claude
    instances (Anthropic). "Fresh-context" referees share the lead's model family, so their agreement is not independent confirmation.
- **Prior art.**
  - S1.k is anticipated by Roos–Praveen (`roos-j/lean-oscillatory`, under stronger regularity).
  - The A3 rate t^{−3/4} is classical (Duistermaat 1974; Arnol'd 1973; cited in `docs/statements.md`, Batch A3,
    "Sources", from records the lead has not read, so the attribution is FOLKLORE there).
  - The lattice rates are classical (Stefanov–Kevrekidis 2005).
  - The new content is the machine-checked statements and, through T0E′, the explicit constant 8(1 + √7)/3, which
    is within a factor 2 of the best constant (`docs/prior_art.md`).
- **S1.k had no route review.** Its general-k proof lies outside the refereed A3 route. It was reviewed after the proof
  (FAITHFUL, SOUND; `review/2026-11-12-A3-post-proof-answers.md`).
- **Known style warnings.** `OscInt/Cusp/Implicit4.lean` raises six linter warnings (style only; recorded as a NIT).
  The proof text was kept as reviewed and replayed.
