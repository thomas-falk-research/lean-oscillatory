# OscInt v0.6.0 — release notes (prepared by the lead; the owner publishes)

**Status of these notes: PREPARED.** The pins below were computed on the release branch's tree on 2026-11-14 and recomputed on 2026-11-15 (UTC) after batch D4U was
added at the owner's decision.
`release-candidate` re-checks them on the release commit (`tools/release_preflight.sh`) and refuses to proceed if
they differ. This paragraph is not part of the release body.

A minor release. Before 1.0.0 a minor release may change statements; this one changes no existing statement's text,
file, commitment or stamp. It sets `proved_by` for the four statements of batch LS, the free lattice Schrödinger group
on ℓ²(ℤ^d), which v0.5.0 shipped stated, frozen and reviewed but not proved, and it adds the two statements of batch
D4U, the D4 limit uniformly over C²-totally bounded amplitude families and over bounded linear offsets, stated,
frozen, reviewed and proved since v0.5.0. All are machine-checked as stated, so all 65 statements are. Machine-checked here means the trust chain of `docs/STATUS.md`;
statement fidelity has been reviewed by AI referees, not yet by a human expert. Toolchain `leanprover/lean4:v4.35.0`,
Mathlib `56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679`, both unchanged from v0.5.0.

## Statement changes

**No existing statement's text changed; two statements are new.** Compared with the `v0.5.0` tag on 2026-11-14, field
for field, the 63 entries of v0.5.0's `stamps/statements.json` are unchanged except for four `proved_by` fields, which
go from `null` to a theorem (batch LS,
`OscInt/Statements/LatticeGroup.lean`, stamp `stamps/20261112T215742Z-d18e0780abdb`, unchanged):
- LS-E `LatticeSchrodingerExistsStatement`: every ℓ² datum has a strong solution of i u′ + Δu = 0
  (`OscInt.API.latticeSchrodingerExists`);
- LS-K `LatticeSchrodingerKernelStatement`: every solution is the convolution with the kernel
  (2π)^{−d} e^{−2idt} K_d(2t, ·), hence unique (`OscInt.API.latticeSchrodingerKernel`);
- LS-D `LatticeSchrodingerDispersiveStatement`: ‖u(t)‖_∞ ≤ min{1, (C*/(2π))^d (2|t|)^{−d/3}} ‖f‖₁ for t ≠ 0
  (`OscInt.API.latticeSchrodingerDispersive`);
- LS-D′ `LatticeSchrodingerDecayStatement`: ‖u(t)‖_∞ ≤ (1 + τ)^{d/3} (1 + |t|)^{−d/3} ‖f‖₁ for every t, τ ≈ 1.852
  (`OscInt.API.latticeSchrodingerDecay`).

Two entries are new (batch D4U, `OscInt/Statements/D4Uniform.lean`, stamp `stamps/20261115T122915Z-33c56db38f65`;
`statement_files` gains this file):
- S4.2U `D4LimitUniformStatement`: at a D4 critical point, t^{2/3} ∫ e^{itφ} A → c_C A(0) uniformly over every family
  of amplitudes that is C²-totally bounded on B̄(0, δ), δ depending on φ only (`OscInt.API.d4LimitUniform`);
- S4.2O `D4LimitOffsetStatement`: the same limit with a linear term y·κ, uniformly for ‖y‖ ≤ R for each fixed R
  (`OscInt.API.d4LimitOffset`).

Every other field is unchanged. `tools/oscint_check.py --statement` on any of the four LS statements now passes where
v0.5.0 reported "stated but not proved in this OscInt"; the two D4U statements did not exist in v0.5.0.

Notes that travel with the release:
- **AI-assisted proofs, disclosed.**
  - The Lean proofs of the nine LS parts and the final file were drafted by an external reviewer with an OpenAI model
    and did not compile as shipped. The lead's proof agents made them elaborate with 32 mechanical changes, with no
    change of route, statement or lemma; the repairs are committed as `docs/replay/2026-11-14-LS-packet-repairs.diff`.
  - Tool names and versions, as far as the record supports them, are in `docs/prior_art.md`.
  - The lead, the proof agents and every referee are Claude instances (Anthropic). "Fresh-context" referees share the
    lead's model family, so their agreement is not independent confirmation.
- **Batch D4U, AI-assisted, disclosed.** The question, a hand proof and unelaborated Lean drafts with the same
  decomposition came from an external research packet produced with an OpenAI model (version not stated), which the
  lead read and elaborated in scratch before writing the statements. The statements were written fresh by the lead;
  the route follows the packet's decomposition; the Lean proofs are the lead's and a proof agent's. The order claim is
  "no Lean proof in the repository before the reviews were answered and stamped", not independence of the proof
  (`review/2026-11-15-D4U-packet-answers.md`). The result is classical in mechanism (uniform estimate on differences, a
  finite net, the modulation family); the new content is the machine-checked statements.
- **Prior art.** The decay rate is classical (Stefanov–Kevrekidis 2005, ℓ¹ → ℓ^∞ decay (1 + |t|)^{−d/3} with a
  non-explicit constant); existence and the kernel identity are classical functional analysis. The new content is the
  machine-checked statements with explicit constants, which are new only through T0E′ and TdE′ (`docs/prior_art.md`).
- **Known style warnings.** `OscInt/Cusp/Implicit4.lean` raises six linter warnings (style only; recorded as a NIT).
- **Release pipeline.** This release is the first to be published with `tools/publish_release.sh`, after a stricter
  preflight and a candidate run that also requires `ci.yml` (CHANGELOG, "Release pipeline").
