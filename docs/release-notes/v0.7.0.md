# OscInt v0.7.0 — release notes (prepared by the lead; the owner publishes)

**Status of these notes: PREPARED.** The pins below were computed on the release branch's tree on 2026-11-19 (UTC).
`release-candidate` re-checks them on the release commit (`tools/release_preflight.sh`) and refuses to proceed if
they differ. This paragraph is not part of the release body.

A minor release. Before 1.0.0 a minor release may change statements; this one changes no existing statement's text,
commitment or stamp. It adds the 21 statements of batches PA, KL, FR, VC2 and HW, stated, frozen, reviewed and proved
since v0.6.0: the exponentially convergent trapezoidal rule with the Fourier-coefficient bound behind it and the
light cone of the lattice kernel; the Kusmin–Landau inequality in Landau's sharp form; the Schrödinger group on a
ring and on an open chain with explicit finite-size bounds; the second-derivative van der Corput bound with its
sharp constant; and the Hadamard walk on the line with an explicit t^{−1/3} bound. All are machine-checked as
stated, so all 86 statements are. Machine-checked here means the trust chain of `docs/STATUS.md`; statement fidelity
has been reviewed by AI referees, among them one of a different model family, not yet by a human expert. Toolchain
`leanprover/lean4:v4.35.0`, Mathlib `56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679`, both unchanged from v0.6.0.

## Compatibility: the checker of this release must be used

Every file under `OscInt/Statements/` and `Challenge/` now ends with a fixed attribution block that names the author
and the licence (`docs/decisions/2026-11-19-attribution-blocks.md`). The frozen text of each statement file is the
bytes before the block; `stamps/statements.json` records the SHA-256 of those bytes, so the 65 values of v0.6.0 are
unchanged. `tools/oscint_check.py` of this release strips the block before hashing. **A copy of the checker taken
from v0.6.0 or earlier hashes the whole file and reports every statement file as edited**; replace it with the one
of this release (its SHA-256 is in the table above). Statement commitments and challenge exports are unchanged; the
tools recompute them and compare them with the stamps.

## Notes that travel with the release

- **AI-assisted proofs, disclosed.** The statements, the proofs, the sanity layers and every review of these five
  batches were written by AI agents of one model family under the owner's direction (the commit trailers record
  it). "Fresh-context" referees share the lead's model family, so their agreement is not independent confirmation.
- **External review.** On 2026-11-19 an AI system of another model family reviewed the five batches, one per
  conversation; it could not run Lean. It found all 21 statements faithful to their prose and true by arguments of
  its own, and the proof routes sound. Its findings were against records, tools and process; each was checked and
  is answered in `review/2026-11-19-{VC2,PA,FR,KL,HW}-external-review-answers.md`. No human expert has reviewed the
  statements.
- **The replay driver was corrected.** Its scan of checker output for errors could be switched off by a checker's
  success phrase, and acceptance was matched as a bare substring. Both are fixed with tests, and the raw output of
  every checker step is now kept. Earlier replay records were made with the old rule; `docs/STATUS.md`, paragraph
  "External reviews of 2026-11-19", says what a rerun with raw output showed.
- **A review rule was waived for six batches.** Findings against the sanity layer were answered by extending it,
  without the re-freeze that `CONTRIBUTING.md` step 5 prescribes; the owner ratified this afterwards as a one-time
  waiver, marked on 23 rows of `docs/STATUS.md` (`docs/decisions/2026-11-19-step5-waiver-six-batches.md`).
- **Statement before proof** holds for Lean proof text. For these batches a proof plan on paper predates the freeze,
  and for VC2 a complete proof on paper does; each batch's paragraph in `docs/STATUS.md` says so.
- **Prior art.** Nothing in the five batches is claimed as new mathematics: the trapezoidal theorem is Trefethen and
  Weideman's Theorem 3.2 (essentially Davis 1959), the Kusmin–Landau inequality is classical in Landau's form, the
  sharp second-derivative constant goes back to Kershner (cited from secondary accounts; the originals were not
  read), the walk's decay rate is in Sunada–Tate and Maeda et al. The new content is the machine-checked statements
  with explicit constants. Not found formalised elsewhere in a finite search, whose limits `docs/prior_art.md`
  states. The references, with identifiers checked against Crossref, arXiv and zbMATH Open, are in
  `docs/references.bib`.
- **Constants are explicit, not always sharp.** HW-K has content only for t > 1679 and HW-D, HW-L only for
  t > 59319; `docs/which-theorem.md`, section "Explicit constants", lists from where each bound beats the trivial one.
- **Attribution and licences.** Copyright 2026 Thomas Falk III; code Apache-2.0, documentation CC BY 4.0. `NOTICE`
  is the attribution file that a redistribution must carry. An SPDX bill of materials (`make sbom`) is attached to
  the release.
- **Known style warnings.** `OscInt/Cusp/Implicit4.lean` raises six linter warnings (style only; recorded as a NIT).
