# Pre-registration: numeric test of batches SPL-N, A5U and LOC2 (draft statements, before the freeze)

Registered 2026-11-28 (session 17), before the program below has been run. The statements under test are the drafts
in `OscInt/Statements/StationaryLeadingSharp.lean` (SPL-NU, SPL-NL; SPL-NK and SPL-NC only through them),
`OscInt/Statements/TriangularCrossover.lean` (A5U) and `OscInt/Statements/TorusLocalizationC2.lean` (K2-FQ), none of
them frozen. They use the frozen definitions `splKernel`, `fresnelPrim`, `fresnelLimit`
(`OscInt/Statements/StationaryLeading.lean`), `triKernel` (`OscInt/Statements/TriangularDecay.lean`), `triPhase`,
`triExceptional` (`OscInt/Statements/TriangularAniso.lean`), `torusBox` (`OscInt/Statements/Global.lean`) and `lin`
(`OscInt/Statements/StationaryPhase.lean`). Numerics are tooling, never evidence for a Lean theorem.

**SPL-N is not an independent discovery.** The rationals `360987/500000` and `72197399/10⁸` and the point `8123/5000`
come from an external research packet. The lead re-ran its exact rational certificate (40 cells for the cap, the
lower point) before the statements were written (`review/2026-11-28-postK2-packet-answers.md`, sections 2 and 3). The
SPL-N lines below check the same numbers by other methods, in floating point. Method (ii) uses a representation of κ
that the lead derived from the frozen definitions (same record, section 3), after reading the packet's route. It is
independent of the packet's code and certificate, not of its mathematics.

**What was known at registration.**
- The packet record (`review/2026-11-28-postK2-packet-answers.md`): the receipts it reproduced, the lead's paper
  checks of the SPL-N route (`κ(x) = x + iLx²/2 − i∫₀ˣ (x − v)² e^{iv²/2}/2 dv`, `|L − F(x)| ≤ 2/x`,
  `|κ − L/2| ≤ 4/x³` for `x ≥ 2`), and the shell count behind the constant of LOC2.
- The K2 registration and its run (`docs/prereg/2026-11-28-K2.md`, `numerics/results-k2-checks-2026-11-28.txt`):
  a band test over `t ∈ [20, 160]` cannot separate the exponents 2/3 and 3/4, and exponent fits over short ranges
  come out steeper than the true rate (referee J's caution J-12).
- Exploration by the drafting agent before the program was written (scratch scripts, output not kept, not results):
  the maximum of `|κ|` near `τ = 1.6246006`, about `0.7219739989980`, so about `1.0e−9` below the cap and `9.0e−9`
  above the lower rational; the local maxima of `|κ|` on `[0, 50]` (the first at `τ ≈ 1.625`; the second, about
  0.6428, near 3.81; the later ones smaller); `x³|κ(x) − L/2|` on `[2, 200]`, largest at `x = 200` and close to 1;
  one lattice evolution at `α = a*` on 16 times of `[1, 150]` (max/median of `t^{2/3} sup|K|` about 1.43; the
  same with `t^{3/4}` about 10 % larger at `t ∈ [55, 150]` than at `t ∈ [10, 28]`).
- A smoke test of the program before registration (each criterion's function run once on part of its data, through a
  stub that loads the program; output not kept, not a result). What it ran and found:
  1. SPL-N on the grid of `[0, 13]` (not `[0, 50]`), 21 of the 200 agreement points, 501 tail points; the controls
     CU, CL, CT on the same data. The 40 → 60 digit change at `x = 200` was `1.7e−31`, against a draft threshold of
     `1e−30`: about 10 digits cancel there. The threshold measures working precision, not the statement; it is now
     `1e−25`.
  2. At `8123/5000` the two methods agree to all 40 digits (difference 0). A margin compared with a difference of 0
     is not decided at finite precision; every decision of SPLN-U, SPLN-L, CU and CL now uses
     `max(difference, 1e−35)`, and the output prints both.
  3. FQ-I with the draft criterion "relative error ≤ 1e−9" failed at grids 512² and 1024² (relative errors up to
     `1.4e−6`), in cases where `|L|` is about `1e−11` (zero phase, `t = 13.3`, high frequency `tp`): the absolute
     errors were `8e−18` to `2e−16`, the rounding level of sums whose terms have size `T`. Diagnosed as rounding, not
     a defect of the identity, so the criterion is now `|L − S| ≤ 1e−9|L| + 1e−14 T` (`T` defined in the program),
     with the number of cases decided by the second term and the smallest `|L|/T` printed (lessons 65b: this
     tolerance was added because a test failed; the diagnosis is the reason it is kept).
  4. The A5U band was chosen from one coupling only: `α = a*`, `ε = 0` (its partner `−a*` has the same moduli), on
     the registered grid of 25 times in `[1, 200]`: max/median 1.386, so `BAND = 2.0`. On the same data the control
     statistic `G` of CA5 was 1.0815 against the threshold 1.05. CA5's outcome on its registered data is therefore
     known at registration. The threshold 1.05 was set after the exploratory run above. A wiring run of the whole
     program with `T_MAX = 20` gave `G = 1.03` (the control did not fail there) and exit code 1; with `BAND = 0.5`
     forced, A5U-S failed and the exit code was 1.
  5. A5U-Q: small-t sites agree to `2.1e−15`, quadrature change `1.4e−15`; the controls CQt and CQd differ by 4.06
     and 3.54. The FFT comparison ran at `t = 50` (difference `6.2e−13`), not at the registered `t = 200`.
  6. A5U-Y came out exactly 0: the evolutions of `α` and `−α` are related by sign flips in every floating
     operation. The line checks the indexing and the pairing of couplings, not accuracy; the program says so.
  7. LOC2 on grids 256 and 512 (not 1024 and 2048): FQ-B decided TRUE in 81 of 81 cases, largest `|L|/(D B_N)`
     0.0315 (the cutoff `χ₃`, `t = 0`); the control with 1/20 decided FALSE in 38 of 81 cases; CFI failed in 54 of
     81. FQ-D (the full data): `2.0e−7`.
  Measured times of the smoke parts (one core, this container): SPL-N parts 5 s; the band coupling 71 s (one pair,
  the full time grid, peak memory 519 MB); A5U-Q parts under 1 s, the FFT at `t = 50` 4 s; the FFT step of 4096²
  alone 1.8 s at peak memory 1.1 GB; LOC2 at 256/512 about 4 s, the identity at 1024 11 s; the whole-program
  wiring run 25 s.

## Protocol (fixed now)

Program `numerics/review/spln_a5u_loc2_checks.py`. Its docstring defines every criterion and control. In brief
(κ = splKernel, `L = fresnelLimit`, `E(v) = e^{iv²/2}`, `K = triKernel` unnormalised, `a* = 1/(2√2)`):

| Line | Statement | Criterion |
|---|---|---|
| SPLN-A | NU, NL | κ by (i) the frozen formula with `mpmath.fresnelc/fresnels` (`F(x) = √π (C + iS)(x/√π)`) and (ii) `x + iLx²/2 − i∫₀ˣ (x−v)² E(v)/2 dv` by `mpmath.quad`, both at 40 digits: difference ≤ 1e−25 at 200 points (`12(i+1)/200`, i = 0..198, and 8123/5000) |
| SPLN-U | NU, NL | grid maximum of `\|κ\|` on `j/1000 ∈ [0, 12]`, refined by `findroot` on `d\|κ\|²/dτ = 2 Re(κ̄ κ′)`, `κ′ = E + iτ(L − F)`: in `(72197399/10⁸, 360987/500000]`, both comparisons decided (margin above `max(method difference, 1e−35)`), refined value ≥ grid maximum, `\|mpmath.diff\|` there < 1e−20 |
| SPLN-G | NU | `\|κ\| ≤ 360987/500000` on `[0, 50]`: step 1e−3, interpolation pad `(\|L − F(a)\| + h) h²/8` per cell, failing cells split by 10 down to 1e−8 (a cell failing there is a FAIL); large τ in exact rationals: `√(22/14)/2 + 4/50³ < cap` (π < 22/7), with the tail bound of the lead's paper check |
| SPLN-L | NL | `\|κ(8123/5000)\| − 72197399/10⁸ > 0` by both methods, decided; value printed to 15 digits |
| SPLN-T | (route) | `\|κ(x) − L/2\| ≤ 4/x³` at 10001 log-spaced points of `[2, 200]`; 40 → 60 digit change at the worst point below 1e−25 |
| A5U-Q | A5U | `(2π)⁻² K(α, t, n) = (e^{itH}δ₀)(n)`, H with hopping 1/2 to `(±1, 0), (0, ±1)` and α/2 to `±(1, 1)`: at `α = a* + 1e−2`, `t = 0.9, 2.3, 4.1`, sites `(3, 1), (−2, 5), (4, −1)`, against the periodic trapezoid sum on 256² (change from 128² < 1e−13), within 1e−10; at `α = a*`, `t = 200`, every box site within 1e−9 of the 4096² FFT kernel |
| A5U-Y | (code) | `K(−α, t, n) = (−1)^{n₁+n₂} conj K(α, t, n)` for every evolved pair and time, within 1e−10 |
| A5U-S | A5U | 18 couplings `a₀ + s ε`, `a₀ = ±a*`, `ε ∈ {0, 1e−3, 3e−3, 1e−2, 3e−2}`, `s = ±1` (as 9 evolved pairs `α, −α`), 25 log-spaced times of `[1, 200]`: `S = sup_n \|K\| / (t^{−2/3} E(ε t^{1/3}))` has max/median ≤ 2.0 over all 450 values; evolution by `scipy.sparse.linalg.expm_multiply` on the box `[−R, R]²` |
| FQ-D | K2-FQ | closed-form `∂₁χ, ∂₂χ, Δχ` of the three cutoffs against central differences at 200 seeded random points each: relative 1e−4 |
| FQ-I | K2-FQ | `∫χ e^{it(Ψ + p·k)} = Σ_n ĥ(n) c(n + j)`, `j = ⌊tp⌋` (floor offsets), full discrete sum on 2048²: `\|L − S\| ≤ 1e−9\|L\| + 1e−14 T` in all 81 cases |
| FQ-B | K2-FQ | `\|L\| ≤ locConstC2(χ) B`, 81 cases (3 cutoffs × 3 phases × `t ∈ {0, 0.7, 13.3}` × 3 velocities), grids 1024 and 2048, every case decided TRUE with the grid changes as error bounds; counts of TRUE, FALSE and undecided printed |

Data of LOC2. Cutoffs: `χ₁` a C^∞ complex bump `exp(−1/(1 − u)) (1 + 0.3k₁ + 0.2ik₂)` on an ellipse; `χ₂ =
(1 − |k − c|²/ρ²)³₊`, `c = (−0.5, 0.7)`, `ρ = 2` (exactly C²); `χ₃` the non-radial product
`(1 − ((k₁ − 0.2)/2.6)²)³₊ (1 − ((k₂ + 0.1)/3)²)³₊` (C², support `[−2.4, 2.8] × [−3.1, 2.9]`). Phases: `triPhase 0.3`;
`triPhase 0.3 + 0.8|sin k₁|` (continuous, not C¹); `0`. Velocities `(0.37, −1.21)`, `(−2.83, 0.52)`, `(1.13, 3.07)`.
Norms and `D = locConstC2 χ` from the closed-form derivatives on the grid. `B_N = max |c(n)|` over `|n|∞ ≤ 511`; the
tail bound `T_N` for `|n|∞ > 511` (contour shift in analytic directions; two integrations by parts across the kinks
of `|sin k₁|`) enters only the decision FALSE, i.e. only the controls.

Resolution arguments (lessons 16, 34). SPL-N: mpmath at 40 digits; at most 10 digits cancel on `[0, 200]` (checked by
the 40 → 60 change). A5U: box radius `R` the least with `(2 + |α|)√(8R) e^{−μR + T c sinh μ}/(c sinh μ) < 1e−12` for
some μ on a grid of `(0, 8]`, `c = 1 + |α|`: Duhamel's formula for `e^{itH} − e^{itH_R}` with the Combes–Thomas bound
`|(e^{isH_R}δ₀)(m)| ≤ e^{−μ|m|∞ + s c sinh μ}` (weight `e^{μ m_j}`; the anti-Hermitian part of the conjugated
hopping has norm at most `(1 + |α|) sinh μ` by the Schur test); the same bound makes every site outside the box below
1e−12 (normalised), so the box supremum is the supremum over ℤ². `R` from 334 to 347 at `T = 200` for the 18 couplings (297 to 383 for A5U-W).
The a-posteriori checks are the unitarity defect, A5U-Q's small-t quadrature and its large-t FFT comparison. LOC2:
grid change `M → 2M` (1024 → 2048) of every `L`, every coefficient with `|n|∞ ≤ 511` and every `D`, used as the error
bound of the 2048 value; this is an estimate, not a proof (for errors decaying like `M^{−2}` or faster the true error
of the finer value is at most a third of the change, once in the asymptotic regime).

Controls, each of which must FAIL its criterion, and only on a decided violation:
- CU: SPLN-U with cap 0.7219739 (the refined maximum exceeds it by more than the decision threshold);
- CL: SPLN-L with target 0.721974 (the value is below it by more than the threshold);
- CT: SPLN-T with 4 replaced by 1/2;
- CQt: A5U-Q with `e^{−itH}`; CQd: A5U-Q with the diagonal hopping on `±(1, −1)` (each: some site differs by more
  than 1e−6). The sign of `n·k` cannot be controlled: `triPhase α (−k) = triPhase α k` gives `K(α, t, n) =
  K(α, t, −n)`, so both signs give the same values; the program prints both quadratures;
- CA5: at `ε = 0`, `E ≡ 1` and the rate 3/4: `S′ = t^{3/4} sup_n |K|` must grow: at both `a₀`,
  `G = mean S′(t ≥ 50) / mean S′(6.25 ≤ t ≤ 25) ≥ 1.05` (an exact `t^{−2/3}` law gives about `8^{1/12} = 1.19`). The
  band criterion itself does not reject the rate 3/4 at these times; its value for `S′` is printed;
- CFQ: FQ-B with the constant 4 replaced by 1/20: at least one case decided FALSE;
- CFI: FQ-I with the index `n − j`: at least one case with `|L − S| > 1e−3|L| + 1e−14 T`.

Information only (not part of OVERALL): A5U-W, the A5U-S ratio for `ε ∈ {0.1, 0.2}`, where `ε t^{1/3}` reaches 1.17
and the envelope's second branch is used. The radius `r` of A5U is existential, so no value there can contradict it.

**Overall PASS** iff every criterion line PASSes and every control comes out as required. The program exits with code
1 otherwise.

**Outcomes and what follows, fixed now.**
- PASS: no numerical objection to the draft statements SPL-NU, SPL-NL, A5U and K2-FQ; the freeze may proceed after
  the sanity layer and plants.
- A FAIL of SPLN-U, SPLN-G or SPLN-L with a decided comparison against the statement is a numerical contradiction of
  the packet's certificate as the lead re-ran it. It stops SPL-N until explained: either a defect of this program,
  or the certificate does not prove what was read from it.
- A FAIL of SPLN-A, SPLN-T, A5U-Q, A5U-Y, FQ-D or FQ-I is a defect of the test or of a paper step (for SPLN-T, the
  tail bound of the route), to be explained before the freeze.
- A FAIL of A5U-S is to be explained before the freeze. On its own it is not a counterexample: `C` and `r` are
  existential and the data are finite.
- A FAIL of FQ-B with a case decided FALSE is a counterexample to K2-FQ, unless explained as a defect of the program.
  An undecided case is a defect of the resolution. Either is explained before the freeze.
- A control that does not fail means its criterion does not discriminate; that is a defect of the test, to be
  explained before the freeze. CA5 is the weakest (smoke margin 1.0815 against 1.05).

**What the test cannot show.**
- Floating-point values, no enclosures. The grid of SPLN-G with its interpolation pad uses 40-digit values, not
  enclosures, and the large-τ part rests on the paper tail bound beyond `[2, 200]`. The evidence for SPL-NU and
  SPL-NL is the exact certificate; this test is a cross-check with other methods, and its margins are small
  (`1.0e−9` and `9.0e−9` in the exploration). SPL-NK and SPL-NC are not tested; they follow on paper from SPL-I and
  SPL-A with SPL-NU and SPL-NL.
- A5U: with `ε ≤ 3e−2` and `t ≤ 200`, `ε t^{1/3} ≤ 0.18`, so `crossEnvelope = 1` at every point of the criterion.
  The line tests a `t^{−2/3}` bound with one constant near `±a*`, not the crossover to `ε^{−1/4} t^{−3/4}`, which
  starts near `t = ε^{−3}`. It cannot separate the exponents 2/3 and 3/4 (the band passes either), and the growth
  control is weak. The couplings near `−a*` are the exact images of those near `+a*`, so they add no data. Finite
  data cannot falsify an existential `C` and `r`.
- LOC2: finitely many cutoffs, phases, times and velocities; error bounds a posteriori; the constant is about 30
  times the largest ratio seen in the smoke test, so FQ-B checks the inequality, not the size of the constant. The
  support condition (plant F-a) is not exercised.
