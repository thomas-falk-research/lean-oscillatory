# Pre-registration: second numeric test of the two-saddle statement TSD-C (the line route of case C9)

Registered 2026-11-20 (session 14), after the first registered test (`docs/prereg/2026-11-20-TSD-core.md`) was
run and before the program below has been run. A new registration with its own date, as the first one requires;
the first run's output is kept unchanged (`numerics/results-tsd-core-checks-2026-11-20.txt`, OVERALL: FAIL).

What was already known when this was registered: the whole output of the first run. Its criterion (a), the claim
under test, held in all nine cases, and so did (h) and the controls; (q) and (im) failed in case C9 only (largest
disagreement 7.91e-07 G, `Im J` 2.53e-07 G, against 1e-08 G). So this registration is made with knowledge of
every number except those of the 40-digit route below, and it is not an independent test of (a): it exists to
decide whether the failure in C9 is one of floating-point resolution, as the lead infers, or a disagreement of
the routes. The inference: in C9 the amplitude at the saddle is sech(20), so `G` is about 1e-10, while the
integrand on the real line has size 1 near the origin; binary64 leaves an absolute error near 1e-17 there, which
is 1e-7 G. The first run printed only the largest disagreement of a case, so it does not say which route
disagreed.

## Protocol (fixed now)

Cases, families, parameters, window, formulas, criteria (h), (a), (q), (im) with the same tolerances, controls K1
and K2 with the same thresholds, and the routes R2 and R3: exactly those of the first registration, computed by
the first program's own functions (the second program loads `numerics/review/tsd_core_checks.py` and refuses to
run unless its SHA-256 is the registered c057a781a5dd1db0d09dd48159369c2569d8235d0de3e5299e1de41e6ea0201e).
Two differences:

1. Every disagreement is printed by itself: R2 against its refinement, R3 against its refinement, R2 against R3,
   R1 against its refinement, R1 against R2.
2. In case C9 the route R1 is computed with mpmath at 40 decimal digits: the same rule for the panels, 12-point
   Gauss–Legendre nodes and weights computed to that precision (Newton's method on the Legendre polynomial;
   the program prints the sum of the weights minus 2), and the truncation `X = 60/κ` in place of `40/κ` (the
   neglected tails of `sech(20v)` are then below 1e-26, against 4e-19 before). R2 and R3 stay in binary64: on
   the contour the integrand is of the size of the amplitude at the saddle, and no cancellation of that kind
   occurs.

Overall PASS iff every criterion and every control line is PASS, as before. Outcomes and what follows from them,
fixed now: PASS means that the three routes agree in all nine cases to the registered tolerance and that the
first run's failure was one of resolution in the line route of C9; the statement goes on to its Lean text. A
FAIL of (q) or (im) in C9 with the 40-digit route means a disagreement that is not explained; no Lean statement
is drafted until it is. A FAIL of (a) anywhere would contradict the first run and is a defect of one of the two
programs.

## Program

`numerics/review/tsd_core_checks2.py` (Python 3 with NumPy and mpmath; the versions are printed by the run),
output saved verbatim under numerics/ as results-tsd-core-checks2-2026-11-20.txt (the file does not exist at
registration). The SHA-256 of the program at registration is recorded in `docs/prereg/LEDGER.md`.
