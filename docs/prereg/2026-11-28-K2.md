# Pre-registration: numeric test of batch K2 (draft statements, before the freeze)

Registered 2026-11-28 (session 16), before the program below has been run. The statements under test are the drafts
in `OscInt/Statements/TriangularDecay.lean` (not frozen). Numerics are tooling, never evidence for a Lean theorem.

**What was known at registration.**
- The external packet's receipts and the lead's own checks (`review/2026-11-28-K2-packet-answers.md`, sections 3
  and 4).
- Route referee J's numerics (same file, section 6), in particular its caution J-12: exponent fits of
  `sup_n |K|` over short time ranges come out steeper than the true rate because of preasymptotic terms.
- A smoke test of this program before registration (each criterion's function run once on part of its data,
  through a stub that loads the program; output not kept, not a result). It found three defects of the draft,
  fixed before registration:
  1. The Fourier identity was truncated at `|j| ≤ 40`, where the coefficients of a compactly supported bump have
     not yet decayed. It is now the full discrete sum, which tests the index sign and the normalisations; the grid
     change tests the discretisation.
  2. Fitted exponents for the local model phases missed the target at a cusp (slope −0.56 against −0.75 for
     t ≤ 640). The approach to `t^{−3/4}` there is slow. The criterion is now non-growth of the scaled supremum.
  3. The lattice criterion's exponent fit failed at α = 1 (−0.86 against −0.75 over t ∈ [20, 160]), as J-12
     predicts. The criterion is now a bounded band, which cannot separate the exponents 2/3 and 3/4; the test says
     so.

## Protocol (fixed now)

Program `numerics/review/k2_checks.py`. Its docstring defines every criterion and control. In brief:

| Line | Statement | Criterion |
|---|---|---|
| MC | K2-MA | `Re C ≥ 1/(12m)` for m = 2..40 from the closed form `C = (2/m)Γ(1/m)·(e^{iσπ/(2m)} or cos(π/(2m)))`; symmetric partial integrals at R = 6, 12 within `4/(m R^{m−1})` of C; quadrature change below 1e−8 |
| MA | K2-MA | `\|I_b(t) − t^{−1/m} C\| ≤ 4/(m t r^{m−1})`, m = 2, 3, 4, 6, 7; σ = ±1; two C^∞ plateau cutoffs; t = 0.5, 3, 17.5, 60; quadrature change below 1e−9 |
| ML | K2-ML | `\|I_b(t)\| ≥ t^{−1/m}/(24m)` at the threshold and at twice it |
| F | K2-F | localised integral = `Σ_j c_j K(t, n + j)` (floor offsets, full discrete sum) to 1e−9 relative; `\|L\| ≤ (Σ\|c_j\|) max\|K\|`; grid change (512 → 1024) of L and of the ℓ¹ norm below 1e−5; three couplings, real t, real p |
| L | K2-L | model phases of orders 3, 4 (sheared) and 6: `t^{1/2+1/m} sup_y \|I\|` at t = 320, 640 at most twice its maximum over t = 20, 40, 80 |
| G | K2-U, K2-D | eight couplings (0, ±a*, −0.8, 1, 1.5, 0.25, 2): `t^β sup_n \|K\|` over t ∈ [20, 160] (15 times) in a band with lo > 0 and hi/lo ≤ 2.5; FFT size from an analytic alias bound below 1e−12, capped at 4096 (a cap is a FAIL) |

Controls, each of which must FAIL its criterion: CMA (remainder constant 4 replaced by 1/20), CML (threshold 96
replaced by 1e−6, at t = 1e−8), CL (the sheared A3 model claimed with m = 2), CG (exponent 0 at α = 1, −0.8), CF
(index n − j in place of n + j).

**Overall PASS** iff every criterion line PASSes and every control comes out as required.

**Outcomes and what follows, fixed now.**
- PASS: no numerical objection to the draft statements; the freeze may proceed after the sanity layer and plants.
- A FAIL of MC, MA or ML is a counterexample to K2-MA or K2-ML, or a defect of the test, to be explained before the
  freeze; the statement is not frozen until it is explained.
- A FAIL of F, L or G is to be explained before the freeze, in the same way.
- A control that does not fail means its criterion does not discriminate; that is a defect of the test, to be
  explained before the freeze.

**What the test cannot show.** Floating-point values, no enclosures; finitely many data; G cannot distinguish 2/3
from 3/4 at these times (referee J's Bessel computation to t = 2048 is the better evidence for that, and it is not
part of this registration); L tests model phases, not every phase.

## Program

`numerics/review/k2_checks.py`. Its output will be saved verbatim as numerics/results-k2-checks-2026-11-28.txt, which
does not exist at registration. The SHA-256 of the program at registration is in `docs/prereg/LEDGER.md`. Expected
run time: under 20 minutes.
