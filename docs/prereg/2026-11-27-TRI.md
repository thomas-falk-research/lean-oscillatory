# Pre-registration: numeric test of batch TRI (the anisotropic triangular lattice phase)

Registered 2026-11-27 (session 15), before the program below has been run, by the lead. The batch is the owner's
decision of 2026-11-27: the classification K1 and the coefficients K3 of
`review/2026-11-26-A5-handoff-answers.md`, for `Ψ_a(x, y) = cos x + cos y + a cos(x + y)`.

**What is already known at registration.** This test is not blind; it confirms known numbers.
- Checker C's floating-point results: the A5 point, decay slopes, and the plateau of `t^{2/3} max |K|` at
  `a* = 1/(2√2)` along the anti-diagonal (about 0.341 at t = 8192 and 0.337 at t = 131072).
- Referee F's 40-digit results: 322 random points with no mismatch; `g₆ = −0.0147313912747`.
- The lead's exact SymPy results: the identities and the sextic `−u⁶/(48√2)`.

The program is new and written by the lead. It works in the original coordinates `(x, y)`, from the phase, and
does not use the `(s, v)` algebra of the proof route. A draft of it was read and found defective before any run
(signs for a < 0, a useless Bessel tail bound, a coarse step); the defects were fixed before this registration.
No version of the program has been run.

## Protocol (fixed now)

Registered values of a: 0.05, 0.2, 0.3, 0.4, 0.5, 0.7, 1, 2, −0.3, `a*`, `−a*`. Precision: mpmath, 50 digits.

- **T1 (types and coefficients).**
  - Method: for each registered a, solve `det H = 0` and `D³Ψ[v, v, v] = 0`, with v the unit kernel vector, by
    Newton's method from a 24 × 24 grid of starts on the torus, and deduplicate the solutions. For each solution,
    compute the reduced coefficients `q₄` and `q₆` of the phase on its transverse critical curve. The curve is
    found by solving the transverse equation, after removing the linear part at the point. The coefficients are
    read off by interpolation of degree 8 at 9 points with step 1e−3; by stationarity the values are accurate to
    the working precision.
  - PASS iff all four hold:
    1. for a ≠ ±a*, every solution has `|q₄| > 1e−8`;
    2. for a = ±a*, exactly two solutions have `|q₄| < 1e−12`. They lie at `(θ, −θ)` modulo 2π with
       `cos θ = −2a` (for a < 0, after the translation by `(π, π)`), and there `q₆ = −sign(a)/(48√2)` to 1e−10;
    3. the matching-sign points `(±π/2, ±π/2)`, translated by `(π, π)` for a < 0, are solutions with
       `q₄ = −1/(32a)` to 1e−12;
    4. for 0 < |a| ≤ 1/2, the anti-diagonal point is a solution with `q₄ = (8a² − 1)/(32a)` to 1e−12.
- **T2 (decay at a*).**
  - Quantity: `r(t) = t^{2/3} max |K_{a*}(t, (n₁, −n₁))|` over every integer `|n₁| ≤ t`. It is computed by the exact
    Jacobi–Anger series in binary64 (SciPy `jv`), with the m-sum truncated at
    `M = ⌊|ta| + 40 + 10|ta|^{1/3}⌋`. The tail is bounded by `|J_m(z)| ≤ exp(|z| sinh η − |m|η)` with
    `η = acosh((M + 1)/|z|)`, and `|J_j(t)| ≤ 1` for the other two factors.
  - PASS iff `r(16384)/r(4096)` and `r(65536)/r(16384)` lie in [0.97, 1.03], and the tail bound is at most
    1e−12.
- **K1 (control).** The same quantity at a = 0.2. PASS iff `r(16384)/r(4096) < 0.95`: faster than `t^{−2/3}` along
  that line. The A3 rate gives about 0.89, and the crossover profile at `a t^{1/3}` from 3.2 to 5.1 gives about
  0.86.
- **K2 (control).** At a = 0, the points of Hessian rank 0, by the same Newton method from a 16 × 16 grid. PASS iff
  exactly four are found, at `(±π/2, ±π/2)`.

Overall PASS iff every line is PASS.

**Outcomes and what follows, fixed now.**
- PASS: the statements of batch TRI are written as planned.
- A FAIL of T1 (i) or (ii) is a counterexample to K1 or K3 and stops the batch until it is explained.
- A FAIL of T1 (iii) or (iv) is a defect of the program or of a formula, to be explained before any statement.
- A FAIL of T2 or of a control is a numerical-resolution question, answered by a new registration. It is not
  evidence about K1.

**What the test cannot show.** A search from a finite set of starts is not a proof that all points were found;
completeness is the job of K1's proof. Floating-point values in T2 are not enclosures.
