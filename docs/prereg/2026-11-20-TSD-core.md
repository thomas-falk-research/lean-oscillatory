# Pre-registration: numeric test of the two-saddle statement TSD-C

Registered 2026-11-20 (session 14), before the test program has been run and before any Lean statement or proof
of batch TSD exists. Numerics are tooling, never evidence for a Lean theorem (README); this test exists to catch
a wrong statement (a constant, a sign, the phase factor of the leading term, a missing hypothesis, an error in
the lead's transcription of the statement into the form of `docs/statements.md`) before it is frozen. Changing
the protocol after the run is a new registration with its own date; the first run's output is kept.

What was already known when this was registered. Three readings of the source's proof found the lemma true, two
of them with numerics of their own on other instances (1526 random and about twenty chosen instances; 226
instances; eight instances; `review/2026-11-20-TSD-step-referee-answers.md`). Their programs did not survive
session 13's container and are not used here. The program below was written by the lead in this session and
compiled (`py_compile`), not run; no case of this protocol had been computed by it. The lead estimated the four
error terms of several cases by hand while choosing the parameters (to make the bound smaller than 1 in most
cases, so that the controls can discriminate), and changed the parameter `t` of C4, C7 and C9 for that reason
before registration. No integral had been computed.

## Claim under test (`docs/statements.md`, "Batch TSD", TSD-C)

Under hypotheses (1) to (7) there: `J = ∫_ℝ A(v) e^{itφ(v)} dv` exists, is real, and
`|J − lead| ≤ G (ε_core + 2 ε_tr + ε_mid + ε_far)` with `lead = 2 √(2π/(tα)) Re[e^{−iπ/4} A(s) e^{itφ(s)}]`,
`G = 2 |A(s)| √(2π/(tα))` and the four terms as written there, with `K = (e − 2)(M² + M′)`.

## Families (fixed now)

Phases, both odd, real on ℝ and entire or analytic on the strips used:
- P1(σ): `φ(z) = σ² z − z³/3`. `s = σ`, `α = 2σ`, `β = σ`; `C₂ = 2`, `C₃ = 0` exactly. Hypothesis (4):
  `φ′(v) = (σ − v)(σ + v)`.
- P2(x), `x > 1`: `φ′(z) = x − √(1 + z²)`, `φ(0) = 0`. `s = √(x² − 1)`, `α = s/x`, `β = (x − 1)/s` (chord and
  tangent of a convex function). For `2δ² ≤ 1`: `C₂ = (1 − δ²)^{−3/2}`, `C₃ = 3/(1 − δ²)²`, upper bounds that
  follow from `|1 + z²| ≥ 1 − δ²` and `|z|² ≤ |1 + z²|` on the strip.

Amplitudes, all with `A(−z̄) = conj A(z)`, integrable on ℝ and without zeros on the strips used:
- A1(κ): `sech(κz)`, `κδ < π/2`. `M = κ max{1, tan κδ}`, `M′ = κ² sec² κδ` (exact suprema).
- A2(γ, κ): `(1 + iγz) sech(κz)`, `|γ|δ < 1`. `M = |γ|/(1 − |γ|δ) + κ max{1, tan κδ}`,
  `M′ = γ²/(1 − |γ|δ)² + κ² sec² κδ` (upper bounds).
- A3(a): `a²/(a² + z²)`, `a² ≥ 3δ²`. `M = 1/√(a² − 2δ²)`, `M′ = 2/(a² − δ²) + 1/(a² − 2δ²)` (upper bounds).

The window is the largest admissible one: `ℓ = min{√2 δ, α/C₂, (3√2/2) β/C₂, 1/M}`, `u = ℓ/√2`,
`r = min{ℓ, (6/(tC₂))^{1/3}}`.

## Method (fixed now)

IEEE-754 binary64, round to nearest. Composite Gauss–Legendre quadrature, 12 nodes per panel; the width of a
panel that starts at the point `z` is `min{0.02, 1/(t |φ′(z)| + 1)}`, so that `tφ` changes by about one radian at
most across a panel. Three routes for `J`:
- R1 (cases with A1 or A2 only): the real line from `−X` to `X`, `X = 40/κ` for A1 and `48/κ` for A2; the
  complex value is kept, so that `Im J` is measured.
- R2: twice the real part of the right half of the contour of the source's proof with the window `ℓ`: the line
  at height `u` from 0 to `s − u`, the segment `s + ρ e^{−iπ/4}`, `|ρ| ≤ ℓ`, and the line at height `−u` from
  `s + u` to `s + u + 40/(tαu)`.
- R3: the same with the window `ℓ/2`.
Every route is computed a second time with half the panel width. The value used for `J` is R2 at the finer
width. No random numbers. R1 against R2 tests the deformation itself; R2 against R3 tests the two contour
integrals against each other where R1 is not available (A3).

## Criteria (fixed now)

- (h) in every case C1 to C9 the inequalities of hypotheses (6) and (7) hold for the computed parameters
  (relative tolerance 10⁻¹²; C8 has two of them at equality). Hypotheses (1) to (5) hold by the construction of
  the families and are not tested by the program.
- (a) `|J − lead|/G ≤ ε_core + 2 ε_tr + ε_mid + ε_far + 10⁻⁹` in every case.
- (q) every route agrees with its refinement, and the routes agree with each other, within `10⁻⁸ G`.
- (im) `|Im J| < 10⁻⁸ G` in the cases with route R1.

Controls, each of which must come out as stated or the run is NOT DISCRIMINATING:
- K1: with `e^{+iπ/4}` in place of `e^{−iπ/4}` in the leading term, the error exceeds the bound in at least 4 of
  the 9 cases.
- K2: with `α/2` in place of `α` in the leading term only, the error exceeds the bound in at least 4 of the 9
  cases.
(The number 4 and not 9: the bound exceeds 1 in C8 by the lead's estimate, and a wrong leading term can be close
to the right one when the phase of the saddle contribution is unfavourable.)

Overall PASS iff every criterion and every control line is PASS. Any other outcome is reported as it is, with the
numbers; the statement in `docs/statements.md` is then revised before any freeze, and the revision cites this
record. What a PASS does not show: that the hypotheses are the right ones (no case violates one, except I1);
anything about amplitudes or phases outside the two families each; sharpness of any constant.
