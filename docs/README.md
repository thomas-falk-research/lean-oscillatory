# Documentation index

Start with the repository `README.md`. For a working session, read `lessons.md` (binding rules), then `HANDOFF.md`.
What is proved is in `STATUS.md` (derived from evidence); each statement's mathematics is in `statements.md`.

## Where to start

| You want to | Read |
|---|---|
| use a theorem in your own Lean project | `which-theorem.md`, then `cookbook.md` and `downstream.md` |
| know exactly what a statement says | `statements.md` (prose and modelling choices), then the Lean text in `OscInt/Statements/` |
| judge how far to trust a result | `STATUS.md` (evidence per statement), then "Known caveats" in the repository `README.md` |
| know what is classical and what was found elsewhere | `prior_art.md`, `references.bib` |
| see where the estimates apply | `applications.md` |
| re-check everything yourself | "How it is checked" in the repository `README.md`; `toolchain.md` |
| add a statement or change a proof | `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `lessons.md` (binding rules), `policy.md` |
| continue a working session | `lessons.md`, then `HANDOFF.md` |

## Labels used throughout

The exact text and status of each label are in `statements.md` and `STATUS.md`.

- **S1.1–S1.3**: one-dimensional van der Corput bounds (first, second, third derivative), with amplitude.
- **S1.k**: the k-th derivative van der Corput bound.
- **S2.1**: non-stationary phase.
- **S2.2**: nondegenerate stationary phase, decay `m^{−1}`.
- **S3**: the uniform fold bound, `m^{−5/6}`.
- **S4.1, S4.1′**: the uniform D4 bound, `t^{−2/3}`.
- **S4.2–S4.4**: the D4 limit, the positivity of its constant, and the closed forms of the constant.
- **S5.1, S5.1′**: the uniform cusp (A3) bound, `t^{−3/4}`.
- **F1, F2**: the first front ends (`ContDiff` amplitudes; the D4 limit with its constant).
- **C1–C10, C7′**: conversions from textbook notation (derivatives, Hessians, discriminants, norms, support).
- **FE-…**: front ends restating S2–S4 for users. **…L** and **…l**: their local-smoothness forms.
- **S2.2F, S3c**: the compact-family forms of S2.2 and S3.
- **GD-C, GD, GD-F, GD-N**: global decay from a pointwise classification.
- **SH**: sharpness.
- **T1**: torus unfolding.
- **T6, T6L**: the 2D lattice-Laplacian dispersive bound and its leading term.
- **T0E, T6F, T6E** (batch LE): explicit lattice constants.
- **GD-…-V** (batch VT): the global decay estimates for amplitudes in a Banach space.
- **T0E′, T6E′** (batch LE2): the cube-root constant 8(1 + √7)/3 for the 1D and 2D lattice kernels.
- **TdF, TdE′** (batch HC): the d-dimensional hypercubic lattice kernel factorises; its bound (8(1 + √7)/3)^d t^{−d/3}.
- **LS-E, LS-K, LS-D, LS-D′** (batch LS): the free lattice Schrödinger group on ℓ²(ℤ^d), its kernel representation,
  and its dispersive decay (machine-checked as stated 2026-11-14, replay of 35dc5c0).
- **S4.2U, S4.2O** (batch D4U): the D4 limit uniformly over C²-totally bounded families of amplitudes, and over
  bounded linear offsets.
- **PA-C, PA-T, LC-1** (batch PA): Fourier coefficients of a periodic function analytic in a strip; the trapezoidal
  rule for such a function; the light cone of the 1D lattice kernel.
- **KL-D, KL-C, KL-F** (batch KL): the Kusmin–Landau inequality for exponential sums, on differences with the bound
  cot(πλ/2), with the constant 2/π, and in the derivative form.
- **FR-K, FR-U, FR-S, FR-P** (batch FR, ring of N sites): the ring kernel, that it is the propagator, the method of
  images, the finite-size error against the line. **FC-K, FC-U, FC-P** (batch FR, open chain of M sites): the chain
  kernel, that it is the propagator, the finite-size error against the line.
- **VC2-S, VC2-A, VC2-N, VC2-K** (batch VC2): the second-derivative van der Corput bound with its sharp constant, its
  attainment by the quadratic phase, the bound 7/2 for the profile, and the test with amplitude with 7/2 in place of 8.
- **HW-R, HW-K, HW-D, HW-L** (batch HW): the Hadamard walk on the line in terms of a scalar kernel, the kernel bound,
  the decay t^{−1/3} from a point state, and the dispersive estimate ℓ¹ → ℓ^∞.
- The 21 statements of batches PA, KL, FR, VC2 and HW are new in release v0.7.0.
- **Batch labels:** **API1–API3** are the front-end batches; **GD, LE, LE2, VT, HC, A3, LS, D4U, PA, KL, FR, VC2, HW**
  are the batches named after their statements.

## Process and history

| File or folder | What it is |
|---|---|

| `decisions/` | Owner decisions and the memos behind them |
| `design/` | Design notes (torus lattice, vector amplitudes, leading terms) |
| `prereg/` | Pre-registered numerical studies and their ledger (`prereg/LEDGER.md`) |
| `replay/` | Records of full replays (`make verify-all`) and of planted controls |
| `release-notes/` | One file per release, with the pins of its trust chain |
