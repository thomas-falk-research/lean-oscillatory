## VdC1Statement (S1.1, constant 3)

**1. Verdict**
- **Against docs: FAITHFUL.** Every clause in the docs appears in the Lean, and nothing is added:
  - `HasDerivOn φ φ'` and `HasDerivOn φ' φ''`;
  - `MonotoneOn ∨ AntitoneOn` on `Ioo a b`;
  - `ν ≤ |φ' x|` on `Ioo`;
  - `AmplitudeOn`;
  - the bound `3/ν · (|ψ b| + ∫|ψ'|)`.
- **Against the classical statement: FAITHFUL-WEAKER in the phase, slightly more general in the amplitude.**
  - Stein (VIII §1.2, Prop. 2(a) and Corollary, c₁ = 3) needs only that φ′ is monotone. The Lean also asks that φ′ is differentiable on (a, b) and continuous on [a, b]. Both are documented modelling choices.
  - The amplitude only needs ψ continuous on [a, b], differentiable on (a, b), with ψ′ integrable. Stein assumes ψ smooth.
  - The statement is true. The usual proof applies:
    - Write F(x) = ∫_a^x e^{iφ}. Integration by parts gives |∫ e^{iφ}ψ| ≤ sup|F| · (|ψ(b)| + ∫|ψ′|). The FTC needed here is confirmed in Lean above.
    - 1/φ′ is monotone and continuous on [a, b], so the plain bound is ≤ 3/ν on every subinterval.

**2. Vacuity hunt**
- **Satisfiable together:** yes. `sanity_hypotheses_satisfiable` gives non-degenerate data on [0, 1].
- **Division:** `3 / ν` is only reached under `0 < ν`.
- **`oscInt`:** a genuine integral of a continuous function, with `a ≤ b` so the orientation is the standard one.
- **`ampNorm`:** at least 0, with an integrable integrand.
- **Conclusion:** not trivially true. The left side can be nonzero and the right side is finite.
- **Quantifier order:** everything is universally quantified up front, and the constant is a numeral.
- **`φ''`:** it is used only to say φ′ is differentiable on Ioo. No continuity or integrability of `φ''` is assumed, which matches the docs.
- **Linear term:** S1.1 has none, correctly. Adding one would change the hypotheses on φ′.

**3. Ill-conditioned regimes**
- **a = b:** Ioo is empty, so all hypotheses about interior points hold vacuously. The conclusion becomes 0 ≤ (3/ν)·‖ψ b‖, which is true (proved in Lean).
- **ν → 0:** the bound blows up. That is correct and not vacuous.
- **φ″ unbounded at an endpoint:** allowed. For example φ′ = 1 + √x on [0, 1] satisfies `HasDerivOn φ' φ''`. The statement stays true.
- **φ′ unbounded at an endpoint:** excluded, because φ′ must be continuous on `Icc`. Since φ′ is bounded below by ν in absolute value, this exclusion costs nothing for k = 1.
- **ψ′ integrable but unbounded** (e.g. ψ = √x): allowed, and the statement stays true.

**4. Findings**
- **NIT** (header, line 28): `derivatives of order < k are continuous on [a, b]`. For S1.1 (k = 1), the Lean also requires φ′ (order 1 = k) to be continuous on `Icc`, through `HasDerivOn φ' φ'' a b`. The docs state this correctly; only the header sentence is inaccurate for k = 1.
- **NIT:** `HasDerivOn φ' φ'' a b` is used only as "φ′ is differentiable on (a, b)", and φ″ is otherwise a dangling variable. This is harmless. It is stronger than Stein's hypothesis, but documented.

## VdC2Statement (S1.2, constant 8, linear term p)

**1. Verdict**
- **Against docs: FAITHFUL.** The phase is φ_p = `fun x => φ x + p * x`, the hypotheses are on φ alone, the lower bound `μ ≤ |φ'' x|` is on `Ioo`, and the constant is `8 / √μ`.
- **Against the classical statement: FAITHFUL**, with one small regularity restriction (see the MINOR finding below). The statement is true:
  - φ″ is a derivative, so by Darboux, |φ″| ≥ μ forces φ″ to have one sign on (a, b). This works with no continuity of φ″ assumed.
  - So φ_p′ = φ′ + p is strictly monotone.
  - The usual split around the set where |φ_p′| ≤ δ, combined with S1.1 on the remaining subintervals, gives 2δ + 6/(μδ). With δ = μ^{-1/2} this is 8/√μ, matching c₂ = 2c₁ + 2 = 8.
  - The amplitude form follows by the same integration by parts as in S1.1.

**2. Vacuity hunt**
- **`8 / √μ`:** reached only under `0 < μ`, so `√μ > 0`. `√` is `prefix:max`, so the parse is as intended (confirmed above).
- **The linear term p:** genuinely arbitrary, universally quantified, and placed after φ. The hypotheses on φ″ do not depend on p.
- **No hidden vacuity:** satisfiable by the sanity data.

**3. Ill-conditioned regimes**
- **a = b:** trivially true, as in S1.1.
- **μ → 0:** the bound goes to ∞. That is correct.
- **φ″ unbounded near an endpoint:** allowed, and the statement stays meaningful.
- **φ′ unbounded at an endpoint** (classically possible, e.g. φ′ = −log(b − x), where φ″ ≥ μ): excluded, because `HasDerivOn φ' φ'' a b` asks for φ′ continuous on `Icc`. Such a case is recovered by applying the statement on [a, d] with d < b and letting d → b: the integrand is bounded, and ampNorm on [a, d] tends to ampNorm on [a, b] by continuity of ψ. So nothing is lost in substance.
- **Unbounded but integrable ψ′:** allowed, and the statement is true.

**4. Findings**
- **MINOR** (`HasDerivOn φ' φ'' a b` in VdC2): requiring φ′ to be continuous on the closed interval excludes classical phases whose first derivative blows up at an endpoint. This is documented, implied by the source's C² assumption, and recoverable by the subinterval limit. So it is a fidelity note, not a defect.
- **NIT** (`(a b μ p : ℝ)` together with `fun x => φ x + p * x`): because φ is universally quantified, p adds no logical strength. The p-version is equivalent to the p = 0 version (one direction checked in Lean; the other comes from substituting φ + p x and φ′ + p). The uniformity in p is therefore automatic. That is correct and harmless; it just carries no extra content beyond making the uniformity explicit.

## VdC3Statement (S1.3, constant 18, linear term p)

**1. Verdict**
- **Against docs: FAITHFUL.** It adds `HasDerivOn φ'' φ''' a b` and `μ ≤ |φ''' x|` on `Ioo`, and drops the bound on φ″, exactly as the docs say "in place of". The constant is `18 * μ ^ (-(1 : ℝ) / 3)`, which parses as 18·μ^{−1/3} (confirmed above).
- **Against the classical statement: FAITHFUL**, with the same regularity restriction as S1.2 (here φ′ and φ″ must be continuous on [a, b]). The statement is true:
  - By Darboux, φ‴ has one sign, so φ″ is strictly monotone.
  - The set where |φ″| < δ is an interval of length ≤ 2δ/μ. S1.2 on the at most two remaining pieces gives 2·8/√δ.
  - With δ = μ^{2/3}, the total is 16μ^{−1/3} + 2μ^{−1/3} = 18μ^{−1/3}, matching c₃ = 2c₂ + 2.

**2. Vacuity hunt**
- **`Real.rpow`:** `μ ^ (-(1:ℝ)/3)` is a genuine real power because μ > 0. The junk branches for zero or negative bases are never reached.
- **Exponent:** it is the real exponent −1/3, not a natural-number exponent or integer division (`(-(1:ℝ)/3) = -1/3` holds).
- **p:** genuinely arbitrary, as in S1.2.
- **Satisfiable:** the sanity data with φ‴ = 1 works.

**3. Ill-conditioned regimes**
- **a = b and μ → 0:** as in S1.2.
- **φ‴ unbounded near endpoints:** allowed.
- **φ′ or φ″ unbounded at an endpoint:** excluded, recoverable by the same subinterval limit.
- **Unbounded but integrable ψ′:** allowed, and the statement is true.

**4. Findings**
- **MINOR** (`HasDerivOn φ' φ'' a b → HasDerivOn φ'' φ''' a b`): this requires φ′ and φ″ to be continuous on the closed interval. It is the same documented restriction as in S1.2, and recoverable by the limiting argument.
- **NIT:** p is logically redundant, as in S1.2.
