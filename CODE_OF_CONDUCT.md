# Code of conduct

This is a small research project. Everyone who takes part in its issues, pull requests and discussions is expected
to keep to a few rules.

- Argue about the mathematics, the code and the records, not about the person. A claim is answered with a
  counterexample, a command, a citation or a question.
- Say what you checked and how. "I ran this and saw that" and "I did not check this" are both welcome; a confident
  statement with nothing behind it is not.
- Report an error where it can be fixed: an issue for a statement, a proof or a record; the private channel of
  `SECURITY.md` for a way around the checks. Reports of errors in this project's own work are treated as help.
- Disclose what wrote a contribution: a person, or an AI tool directed by a person. The project does the same.
- No harassment, no insults, no publishing of anyone's private information.

The owner moderates. A comment or contribution that breaks these rules may be edited, hidden or removed, and a
participant who keeps breaking them may be blocked. To raise a concern privately, write to
<research@thomas-falk-research.com>.
